Gradient descent, in 3D

Predict: will the ball settle or overshoot when α is greater than 2? This surface is L(x,y) = ½x² + ¼y², with gradient (x, ½y).

Why does it move?

Each step subtracts α times the gradient: x′ = x − αx and y′ = y − ½αy. This quadratic has its unique minimum at (0,0). A constant learning rate between 0 and 2 converges. A rate above 2 can increase the loss along the x direction. This is a two-parameter illustration, not a literal view of a neural network's full loss landscape.
